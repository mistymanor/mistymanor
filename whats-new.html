What's New

at Misty Manor

How about a 2-Hour Trail Ride?

Interested in a longer ride? Want to learn to go a bit faster than a walk? After you have done the 1-hour ride this year, consider coming on the Sunday 2-hour ride!

For those who are interested, we will teach you to pick up to a trot on this ride.

To book your spot, please call the ranch: 443-277-4693.

Welcome Fall at Misty Manor!

In October and November, welcomes cooler weather with trail rides that go out at dusk that return the the ranch for an additional hour around a campfire with snacks and drinks! This is a great date ride or for families with older children.

Campfire rides are: October 3, 17 and 31; November 14 and 28. Reserve your spot today!

FALL LUNCH RIDES

October 11 * November 8 * December 7

To book your spot, please call the ranch: 443-277-4693.
